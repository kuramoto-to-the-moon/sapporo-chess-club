  | 9:15 | Pairings |

  | 9:30 | Round 1 starts |

  | Time | Activity |

  |------|---------------------------|

  | 8:50 | Venue opens |

  | 9:10 | Round 4 starts |

  | 12:50 | Round 5 starts |

  | 15:30 | Award ceremony & group photo |

  | 15:50 | Cleanup |

  | 16:00 | Event ends |


  Digital chess clock: Fischer mode, 40 minutes + 30 seconds increment per
  move  

  (If no digital clock is available: analog clock, 40 moves in 1 hour + 20
  minutes sudden death)


  ## 3. Tiebreaks

  No tiebreaks will be used.  

  Awards will be given separately for the Open Class, Class A, and Class B.


  ## 4. Venue

  Kaderu 2.7, Room 1010  

  Kita 2 Nishi 7, Chuo-ku, Sapporo  

  (10‑story building across from the main entrance of the Hokkaido University
  Botanical Garden)  

  About a 15‑minute walk from JR Sapporo Station.


  ## 5. How to Apply

  Please apply by the day before the tournament (Friday, July 17, 2026) using
  the form below:  

  <https://forms.gle/xhAbHYc6zkf6GNCe7>


  If you need to cancel, please contact: sapporochessclub@gmail.com


  The number of registered participants will be updated on Chess-Results.com.  

  Participation fee: 2,000 yen (students: elementary, junior high, high school,
  university, and graduate students pay half price).  

  Please pay at the venue on the day of the event (exact change appreciated).


  ## 6. Eligibility

  Japan Chess Federation (JCF) membership is required.  

  Since this is an official JCF tournament, please ensure you are registered
  beforehand.  

  If you cannot register as a member, you may still participate by adding an
  extra 2,000 yen to the participation fee.


  ## 7. Other Notes

  - Participants who own a digital chess clock are requested to bring it.
